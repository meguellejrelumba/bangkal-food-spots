Eli's Bulaluhan

About Eli's Bulaluhan

Eli's Bulaluhan is a well-known local eatery hidden in Bangkal, Makati, famous for serving hot, comforting bowls of authentic Filipino bulalo. It offers a laid-back, homey atmosphere perfect for taking a relaxing break and enjoying a hearty meal.

I recommend visiting Eli's Bulaluhan because it offers a comforting escape from the fast-paced city life. Whether you're dining alone or sharing a warm broth with friends, it's a great spot to unwind and satisfy your cravings.

Place Details

Location Bangkal, Makati City
Type Eatery / Local Restaurant
Entrance Fee Free (Pay per dish)
Best Time to Visit Late evening or rainy hours
Recommended Activity Casual dining, catching up with friends, and enjoying hot soup
